Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password, along with the expense records you create — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate Spendly: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal expense data with third parties. We may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting) solely to operate the service, and they do not have independent rights to use your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach us at support@spendly.app.
